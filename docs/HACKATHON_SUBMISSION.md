## 3. Elevator Pitch (150 Words — Copy/Paste Ready)
**Pip’s Midnight Nursery** brings Track 1’s canon hero—**Pip, The Skull Baby**—to life as an interactive bedtime workshop and character portfolio. As Junior Dream Engineer at Studio 04, Pip designs soothing lullabies, 1,850K porcelain nightlights, and cloud-delivered dreams so no creature in the valley ever has to sleep in the dark.

Visitors can explore Pip’s identity and 3D WebGL cloud stage (with cursor-tracking amber eye-lights and a 3-tap pink bow Easter egg), inspect 6 studio inventions in a dynamic Bento grid, and play every instrument live: weave custom melodies in the **432 Hz Web Audio Lullaby Lab**, calibrate **Porcelain Nightlights**, fly **Dream Delivery Routes**, stack **Maple Toy Blocks**, flip through an **Illustrated Storybook**, and play a **60fps Starlight Dream Catcher arcade game** with downloadable PNG certificates. Visitors can even pin their own composed lullabies directly onto the **Live Dream Guestbook** wall!

## 4. 90-Second Demo Video Script (For Loom / Screen Recording)

- **0:00 – 0:20 | The Hook & Hero Stage:**
  - Load the page to show the smooth Framer Motion entrance and move your cursor around Pip’s 2D portrait to demonstrate the spring-damped amber eye-tracking.
  - Click **`3D WebGL Stage`** to orbit around Pip’s 3D cloud cushion, click the floating `h-a-X-♡` blocks, and **tap Pip’s pink bow 3 times** to trigger the Easter egg confetti and music-box fanfare.
- **0:20 – 0:45 | Sensory Bar & 90s Whimsy Mode:**
  - Toggle **`Midnight Slumber`** (night mode), **`Nightlight Torch`** (flashlight reveal over the Character Identity margin note), and **`90s Whimsy Mode`** (`PIP_OS v1.99` window frames and scrolling marquee).
- **0:45 – 1:10 | The 7 Interactive Workshops:**
  - Scroll through the **6 Invention Bento Cards** and click into **01. The Lullaby Lab**.
  - Wind the golden key, toggle a few star notes on the 8-step sequencer, and click **`Pin Melody to Guestbook`**.
  - Switch briefly to **`07. Dream Catcher & Cert`**, launch the **Full Game Shell**, catch a few stars with `Space` dash, and click **`Download PNG`** on the Dream Parcel Certificate.
- **1:10 – 1:30 | Story Journey & Live Guestbook:**
  - Scroll through the **4-Chapter Bedtime Story Journey** and finish at the **Nursery Intercom & Live Dream Guestbook**, playing back your attached melody right from the pinned wish card!
